Employee Onboarding 
 
1. AGE RESTRICTION RULE 
- All applicants must be at least 18 years old. 
- Maximum age allowed for onboarding is 65 years. 
 
2. SALARY AND COMPENSATION CONSTRAINTS 
- Minimum allowed basic salary: $3,000. 
- Maximum allowed basic salary: $25,000. 
- Any salary above $15,000 requires VP approval. 
 
3. PROBATION PERIOD RULE 
- Probation period must be a number of months between 1 and 6 months. 
 
4. WORKING HOURS 
- Standard weekly working hours must not exceed 40 hours per week.
